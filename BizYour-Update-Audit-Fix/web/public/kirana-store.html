Billing, stock and udhaar for kirana stores — in one app.

A kirana counter runs on speed and trust: dozens of small bills a day, regular customers on monthly udhaar, and stock that must never quietly run out. BizYour keeps all three in one place — no separate khata book, no second app for billing.

What a kirana store gets

  • Fast counter billing — make the bill in seconds and send it to the customer on WhatsApp as a PDF. No printer needed.
  • Udhaar that collects itself — every customer's credit is recorded clearly, and each customer gets a shareable hisab link showing their own balance — fewer disputes at the month's end.
  • Stock with low-stock alerts — daily essentials get flagged before they finish, so the shelf is never empty at peak hour.
  • Purchases recorded once — supplier purchases enter the same stock automatically — no double writing.
  • GST only if you need it — GST bills and a ready GSTR-1 file for your CA if you are registered; switch GST off if you are not.
  • Works without internet — billing continues offline and syncs when the connection returns.

Buy your stock inside the same app

Business Mart is BizYour's built-in wholesale bazaar, and it is free. Send an inquiry to wholesalers, compare quotes, place the order — and when you mark it received, it becomes your purchase and updates your stock on its own. The buying that fills your shelves and the billing that empties them finally live in one app, with one login and one set of data.

Common questions

My customers mostly buy on monthly udhaar. Will this handle that?

Yes. Udhaar is a core part of BizYour: each customer's credit and payments are tracked with a running balance, and the customer can see their own hisab on a private link, so the month-end total is never a surprise.

I don't have GST. Is this app for me?

Yes. GST can be turned off completely. Many kirana stores use BizYour only for billing, stock and udhaar.

Do I need a printer or a computer?

No. Bills are shared with the customer on WhatsApp as a PDF, and BizYour runs in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer.

What does it cost?

Business Mart is free forever. The Workspace is free for the first 30 days — no card, no automatic charge. After the trial you choose a monthly or yearly plan inside the app; if you wait, your data stays visible in read-only form and nothing is deleted.
