Pricing

Pricing, without the fine print.

BizYour has two sides, and they are priced differently — plainly, the way we would want it told to us.

Business Mart — free

Buying and selling in Business Mart, BizYour's built-in wholesale bazaar, is free for every business — no trial, no end date, no commission charged by BizYour on your deals. Deals in the Mart are contracts between the buyer and the seller; BizYour provides the platform.

Workspace — 30 days free, then a plan

The Workspace (billing, stock, udhaar, purchases, GST reports) is completely free for the first 30 days, starting from the day you set up your workspace. No card is taken, and there is no automatic charge — ever. When the trial ends, you choose a monthly or yearly plan inside the app and pay yourself; nothing is deducted on its own.

If you choose not to pay, nothing bad happens to your data: the Workspace becomes read-only — you can still see and export everything — and Business Mart keeps working. Pay later, and you continue from exactly where you left off. You can also delete your account and your business data yourself, any time, from Settings — with the small legal exceptions explained in the Privacy Policy.

Why no price list on this page yet?

Because we would rather show you the exact plan prices inside the app — where they apply to your account — than publish numbers here that could go stale. What we promise on this page is the structure, and it does not change: Mart free forever · 30-day free trial · no card · no auto-charge · your data stays yours.

Common questions

Do I need a card to start the free trial?

No. No card, no UPI mandate, no payment details of any kind are taken for the trial.

Will I be charged automatically after 30 days?

No. BizYour never charges automatically. After the trial you decide, inside the app, whether to take a monthly or yearly plan.

What exactly happens if my trial ends and I don't pay?

Your Workspace turns read-only: all your bills, stock and records stay visible and exportable, nothing is deleted, and Business Mart keeps working for free. Paying later unlocks writing again in the same account.

Is Business Mart really free, with no catch?

Yes. The Mart is free to use for buying and selling. BizYour does not take a commission on Mart deals; payment and delivery happen directly between buyer and seller.

Can I leave BizYour and take my data?

Yes. Backup and export are built in, and you can delete your account and your business data yourself from Settings → Data. A few records may be kept in anonymised form only where the law requires it or to settle a dispute, as the Privacy Policy explains. Your data is yours; there is no lock-in.
