For Hardware Shops

Billing, stock and contractor udhaar for hardware shops — in one app.

A hardware shop's register is its hardest worker: hundreds of small items, contractors buying on running credit, and job-wise estimates that turn into material bills. When that record lives in a paper bahi, money leaks quietly. BizYour puts billing, stock and contractor hisab in one app.

What a hardware shop gets

  • Billing for a long item list — fast search and quick bills at a busy counter; the bill goes to the customer on WhatsApp as a PDF.
  • Contractor udhaar, clearly kept — each contractor's running balance is visible at a glance, and the contractor sees the same hisab on a private link — settlement day has no arguments.
  • Estimates for jobs — quote a full job's material first; convert the accepted estimate into bills as material goes out.
  • Stock that keeps up — purchases from suppliers enter the same stock; low-stock alerts catch the fast-moving sizes and fittings before they finish.
  • Returns handled cleanly — leftover material returns are recorded against the same bill and stock, not in a side notebook.
  • GST reports ready — GST figures and a GSTR-1 file for your CA come from the same data you billed with.

Buy from wholesalers inside the same app

Business Mart is BizYour's built-in wholesale bazaar, and it is free. Send one inquiry to up to 20 wholesalers, compare quotes with advance and credit terms shown, and order. When the goods arrive and you mark them received, the purchase and stock update themselves.

Common questions

Contractors settle once a month. Can they see their own hisab?

Yes. Every customer, including contractors, gets a private hisab link showing their own purchases, payments and balance — the same numbers you see.

I give job-wise quotations. Can BizYour do that?

Yes. Estimates are built in, and an accepted estimate converts into a bill without re-entering the items.

Do I need a printer or a computer?

No. Bills are shared with the customer on WhatsApp as a PDF, and BizYour runs in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer.

What does it cost?

Business Mart is free forever. The Workspace is free for the first 30 days — no card, no automatic charge. After the trial you choose a monthly or yearly plan inside the app; if you wait, your data stays visible in read-only form and nothing is deleted.
