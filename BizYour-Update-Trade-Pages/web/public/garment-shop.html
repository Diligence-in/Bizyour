For Garment & Clothing Shops

Billing, stock and returns for garment shops — in one app.

A garment shop lives by seasons and trust: stock bought for a season must sell in that season, exchanges must not become arguments, and regular families buy on relationships. BizYour keeps billing, stock, returns and udhaar together, so the season's real picture is always one look away.

What a garment shop gets

  • Fast billing at a crowded counter — quick bills shared on WhatsApp as a PDF — no printer, no queue build-up during sale season.
  • Returns and exchanges, recorded — a returned piece goes back into stock against its bill; the exchange bill stays linked, so stock never lies.
  • Stock with low-stock alerts — see what is moving and what is sitting, and get alerted before a fast design finishes.
  • Season purchases in one place — every supplier purchase is recorded against stock, so season-end truth — what came, what sold, what is left — is exact.
  • Customer udhaar — regular customers' credit and payments stay in a clear ledger with a hisab link they can check themselves.
  • GST reports ready — GST bills, reports and a GSTR-1 file for your CA from the same data; GST can be switched off if you are not registered.

Buy the next season inside the same app

Business Mart is BizYour's built-in wholesale bazaar, and it is free. Send your season requirement to garment wholesalers, compare quotes, and order — received orders become purchases and stock automatically, ready for the season's first bill.

Common questions

Exchanges are daily in my shop. Will stock stay correct?

Yes. Sale returns are a built-in flow: the returned item re-enters stock against the original bill, and the new bill is recorded in the same place.

Can I see what is not selling?

Your stock list and monthly business report card show what moved and what stayed, so the next season's buying is based on your own numbers.

Do I need a printer or a computer?

No. Bills are shared with the customer on WhatsApp as a PDF, and BizYour runs in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer.

What does it cost?

Business Mart is free forever. The Workspace is free for the first 30 days — no card, no automatic charge. After the trial you choose a monthly or yearly plan inside the app; if you wait, your data stays visible in read-only form and nothing is deleted.
