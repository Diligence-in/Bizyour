For Mobile & Electronics Shops

Billing, estimates and stock for mobile shops — in one app.

A mobile shop sells two things at once: high-value phones that need a proper GST bill, and fast-moving accessories that need tight stock control. Add customers who compare three shops before buying, and the counter needs estimates, billing and stock that work together. That is exactly how BizYour is built.

What a mobile shop gets

  • Proper GST bills — full GST invoices with a ready GSTR-1 file for your CA, shared with the customer on WhatsApp as a PDF.
  • Estimates that win the sale — give a written quotation on the spot; when the customer says yes, it converts into the bill without re-typing.
  • Stock for phones and accessories — handsets, covers, chargers and cables in one stock list, with low-stock alerts on the fast movers.
  • Udhaar, recorded properly — part-payments and balances against a customer's purchase stay in one clear ledger, with a hisab link the customer can check.
  • Returns without confusion — sale and purchase returns are handled in the same place, so exchanged pieces never corrupt the stock.
  • Staff logins with limits — counter staff get billing and stock access; profit and reports stay with the owner.

Buy from distributors inside the same app

Business Mart is BizYour's built-in B2B bazaar, and it is free. Ask several distributors for quotes on the same requirement, order from the best one, and the received order becomes your purchase and stock automatically. No separate purchase register, no re-entry.

Common questions

Can I give a customer a quotation before they decide?

Yes. Estimates (quotations) are built in — share the estimate, and convert it into a GST bill in one step when the customer confirms.

My shop sells on udhaar and part-payments. Is that tracked?

Yes. Each customer's udhaar and every payment against it are recorded with a running balance, and the customer can view their own hisab on a private link.

Do I need a printer or a computer?

No. Bills are shared with the customer on WhatsApp as a PDF, and BizYour runs in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer.

What does it cost?

Business Mart is free forever. The Workspace is free for the first 30 days — no card, no automatic charge. After the trial you choose a monthly or yearly plan inside the app; if you wait, your data stays visible in read-only form and nothing is deleted.
