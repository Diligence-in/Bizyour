BizYour — Trade Pages + Pricing Pack (25 Nov 2026)
====================================================
Is pack me kya hai:
- 6 naye trade pages (web/dist + web/public dono me, taaki agli build me na udein):
  kirana-store.html, mobile-shop.html, hardware-shop.html, garment-shop.html,
  electrical-shop.html, cosmetics-shop.html — har page ~500 shabd asal text,
  trade ke dard par, 4 sawal-jawab + FAQ schema ke saath.
- pricing.html — daam wala page (bina number ke: Mart free forever, Workspace
  30 din free, koi card nahi, koi auto-charge nahi, data kabhi hostage nahi).
- sitemap.xml — purani 5 URLs + nayi 7 = kul 12 (live sitemap par hi bana hai).
- llms-full.txt — purani asal content + naye pages ki list aur pricing link.

Push ke steps (hamesha ki tarah):
  robocopy "%USERPROFILE%\Downloads\BizYour-Update-Trade-Pages" "%USERPROFILE%\Downloads\Bizyour-Repo" /E
  cd /d "%USERPROFILE%\Downloads\Bizyour-Repo"
  git add -A
  git commit -m "Trade pages + pricing page + sitemap/llms update"
  git push

Push ke baad:
1. 2-3 minute me Render deploy hoga. Mai khud live check karunga:
   bizyour.in/kirana-store.html aur bizyour.in/pricing.html khulte hain ya nahi.
2. Search Console me sitemap dobara submit karne ki zarurat nahi (wahi file
   update hui hai) — par homepage ka Request Indexing agar abhi tak nahi kiya
   to kar dena; naye pages ke liye chaaho to unke URL bhi inspect karke
   Request Indexing kar sakte ho (ek-ek karke, 2 minute ka kaam).

Note: Ye pages abhi site ke menu/footer se link nahi hain — Google/AI ko ye
sitemap aur llms-full se milenge. Footer me "Shops ke liye" links wala chhota
pack LinkedIn page banne ke saath ek saath dunga, taaki do baar dist na chhedein.
