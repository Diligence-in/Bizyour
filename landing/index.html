Koi bhi samaan wala business

Agar tum samaan bechte ho aur hisaab rakhte ho, BizYour fit hai.

Aksar puche jaane wale sawaal?

Seedhe jawaab, koi chhota akshar nahi.

Iska price kitna hai?

Pehle 30 din bilkul free hain — koi card nahi chahiye. Trial ke baad tum plan chunte ho; tumhara saara data aur kaam waise ka waisa rehta hai.

Kya internet zaroori hai?

Nahi. Billing offline bhi hoti hai — bill bante rehte hain, aur net aate hi data sync ho jata hai.

Kya GST ka kaam hota hai?

Haan. GST bill, poori GST report, aur CA ke liye taiyaar GSTR-1 file — sab usi data se.

Kya mera staff bhi use kar sakta hai?

Haan — staff ko role-limit ke saath apna login milta hai. Wo billing aur stock ka kaam kar sakte hain, par munafa sirf owner ko dikhta hai.

Kya mera data safe hai?

Tumhara data tumhara hi rehta hai. Kabhi bhi backup lo aur jab chaaho apna data export karo — koi lock-in nahi.

Ye kin bhaashaon me chalta hai?

English, Hindi aur Hinglish — kabhi bhi badlo, jo aasan lage.
