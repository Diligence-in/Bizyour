For Electrical Shops

Billing, estimates and stock for electrical shops — in one app.

An electrical shop sells by the piece, the metre and the job: hundreds of small fittings, wire by length, and electricians who take material on credit against a site. BizYour keeps the counter billing, the job estimates and the electricians' hisab in one app, so nothing is left to memory.

What an electrical shop gets

  • Billing across a deep item list — fast search over switches, wires, fittings and fixtures; bills shared on WhatsApp as a PDF.
  • Job estimates first — quote the full material for a wiring job; convert the accepted estimate into bills as material is issued.
  • Electrician udhaar, settled cleanly — each electrician's running balance is recorded purchase by purchase, and he can check the same hisab on a private link.
  • Stock with low-stock alerts — the sizes and brands that move daily get flagged before they run out mid-job.
  • Purchases recorded once — supplier purchases update the same stock — no separate purchase register.
  • GST reports ready — GST figures and a GSTR-1 file for your CA, from the same data; GST off if you are not registered.

Buy from wholesalers inside the same app

Business Mart is BizYour's built-in wholesale bazaar, and it is free. One inquiry can go to up to 20 wholesalers; compare quotes, order, and on receipt the purchase and stock update on their own.

Common questions

Electricians take material against a site and settle later. Is that tracked?

Yes. Their udhaar is recorded against their name with every purchase and payment, and the running balance is visible to both of you — to them, on their private hisab link.

Can I quote a full house-wiring job before starting?

Yes. Estimates are built in for exactly this: list the material, share the quote, and bill from it as the job proceeds.

Do I need a printer or a computer?

No. Bills are shared with the customer on WhatsApp as a PDF, and BizYour runs in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer.

What does it cost?

Business Mart is free forever. The Workspace is free for the first 30 days — no card, no automatic charge. After the trial you choose a monthly or yearly plan inside the app; if you wait, your data stays visible in read-only form and nothing is deleted.
